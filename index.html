IMPACTO (Impact Assessment Ontology) is an ontology for representing impact assessment requirements and processes based on the GDPR and the EU AI Act. IMPACTO specifically represents the Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) and the Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA), assisting stakeholders in determining when assessments are required, structuring assessment processes, and representing concepts such as processing contexts, risks, impacts, locations, personal data categories, and AI capabilities relevant to impact assessments across EU Member State jurisdictions.

The work presented here encompasses both the DPIA and FRIA information requirements. In terms of DPIA conditions, this ontology is the first to gather all the processing activities that require conducting a DPIA and the processing activities that do not require a DPIA as per the Data Protection Authorities of all 30 EU/EEA member states. The full collection of all these conditions (both Required and Not Required) can be found by clicking on the following link: High Risk Categorisations in the GDPR and AI Act

Introduction

The GDPR (Art. 35) and the EU AI Act (Art. 27) impose obligations on controllers and deployers of high-risk AI systems to conduct impact assessments before beginning processing activities or deploying AI systems that are likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons. These obligations require organisations to identify whether their processing activities or AI systems are high-risk and therefore require an assessment, then to document risks and impacts, and implement technical and organisational measures to mitigate identified risks.

IMPACTO proposes an ontology-based approach to represent the concepts and relations required to support both the DPIA and the FRIA processes. It builds on and extends established vocabularies — primarily the Data Privacy Vocabulary (DPV) and its extensions — reusing existing concepts wherever possible and proposing additions to DPV core and to its GDPR, EU AI Act and AI extensions to fill gaps identified through analysis of the GDPR, the EU AI Act, and Member State DPA guidance.

IMPACTO is an ontology expressed using semantic web technologies that represents impact assessment information in a formal, machine-readable, and interoperable manner. It assists stakeholders in determining whether a DPIA or FRIA is required, structuring the content and process of these assessments, and sharing assessment information across jurisdictions and systems.

Requirements

The purpose of IMPACTO is to represent the concepts and relations necessary to support impact assessment processes mandated under the GDPR and the EU AI Act. The primary requirements are to enable organisations to:

  • determine whether a DPIA (GDPR Art. 35) or a FRIA (AI Act Art. 27) is required, including across Member State jurisdictions;
  • structure the process and content of these assessments, including assessing whether the assessment is necessary, processes and outcomes of the assessments;
  • record the outcomes, statuses and notifications associated with each assessment;
  • share assessment information across jurisdictions and systems in a machine-readable and interoperable way.

Overview

Core Concepts and Relations

IMPACTO's new concepts are organised by the assessment they support: DPIA concepts proposed for the DPV GDPR extension (eu-gdpr) and DPV core; general processing context and purpose concepts proposed for DPV core; and FRIA concepts proposed for the DPV EU AI Act extension (eu-aiact), covering the necessity, procedure, outcome, risk and notification steps of AI Act Article 27, together with supporting concepts for deployers, fundamental rights, and FRIA templates and tools.

Wherever DPV and its extensions already provide a concept, IMPACTO reuses it rather than redefining it. The section lists every reused concept. Relations such as dpv:hasStatus, dpv:hasAssessment, dpv:hasImpact and dpv:hasPurpose link the concepts together in impact assessment representations.

Conventions

All new concepts are currently published in the IMPACTO namespace. The prefix in each term names the module to which the concept will be proposed: for example, impacto:eu-aiact-FRIANecessityStatus is intended to become eu-aiact:FRIANecessityStatus. The Proposed for row of each concept states the target module.
